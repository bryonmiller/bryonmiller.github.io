Today was a lazy Saturday. I got up about 8 am (earlier on a Saturday for me), but we stayed home most of the day. I watched some good football games (Alabama beat Florida, barely, to win the SEC Championship) and spent some time with family. I did get a few little chores done, but nothing major. I forgot to mention yesterday that our new roof is up! They stripped everything off and installed a new shingle roof (instead of metal). I'm happy with it so far, but Katelin and I want to change our vinyl siding color now.

We went to Jeana's house tonight for the band Christmas party that we do every year. There were about half as many people this year and it was people that we are around regularly. That made us feel a lot better about being there during this pandemic. Katelin wore her mask most of the time and we tried to socially distance as much as we could. I'm praying God continues to protect us all. It isn't easy making these decisions. The next decision we will have to make is whether or not to have people over for New Years'. This is usually a small get together for us (less than 10 adults counting us), so we may just exclude a few and have them come later to shorten our time. I hate both of those ideas, but I think they may be good ideas.

I'm feeling better about wrapping up the semester at work. I'm still concerned about next semester. I have a lot of work to do to get my classes ready. I will have time during the break to do this, but I don't want to use up a lot of my off-time working on it. I'll need to really knock some things out this next week.

I have a swing set to assemble by Christmas Eve. I think Casey may come over to help some and I might rope Patric into it as well. If I can get everything ready to go, maybe they can help with the big parts on Christmas Eve. We'll see! I hope Alley and Mark have fun with it.
