Sherry's dad died early this morning. That was a sad start to my day. I just hope that Sherry is able to cope with this and that it doesn't lead to health problems of her own. Stress is a big trigger for her. His death also underlines an already apparent health crisis in America. Between him, my grandparents (who are doing about the same), and the continually updated statistics for our area, I am even more aware of how serious this is for us. I haven't changed my behavior that much because I already mask up, socially distance, and limit my exposure to other people. I am more aware though and more sympathetic.

We kept Mark home again today and Katelin worked from home with me. He got a little better today and I think he will be good enough to go to school tomorrow. His cough may linger around for another week or so though. I just hope daycare doesn't send him home. Luckily, we are just two days from the weekend now.

Our SpaceX hoodies came in today and I wore mine. It says "Occupy Mars" on the front. It was fitting that it came in because they actually launched their SN8 starship prototype today after multiple reschedules. Alley, Mark, and I watched it. It was a good flight and a good test. Unfortunately, it didn't land properly, but they expected that to happen. Instead, we got to see a big explosion! Rocket launch watching is becoming a hobby for us.

I got a lot of grading done today and completed an application for our new short-term certificate in Google IT Support. It was a pretty productive day. I still have a lot of grading left to do tomorrow though. I think I can get it done though. We're hoping for some normalcy tomorrow.
