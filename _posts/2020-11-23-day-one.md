I'm laying in bed with Katelin and Alley. Mark is in his Pack 'n' Play and the end of the bed. We had another good day today. I got up a little earlier today and we had fried bologna, eggs, and biscuits for breakfast. I have eaten so much food and candy on this trip! After breakfast we all got ready and went for a drive. We stopped at the covered bridge, walked across it, checked out the stream, found some cool rocks (and we're pretty sure some petrified wood), and then headed to our next spot.

We went all the way to the end of the Greenbriar Ridge road, found a huge, mostly flat rock, and had a picnic. As a cycling passer-by shouted to us, "BEST PICNIC SPOT IN THE WORLD!" I have to say, I think he was right. It was overlooking the river with trees all around. Some evergreens here and there, a few trees still had some autumn colored leaves, and others were bare. The water was low, but rushing around the smooth rocks and mossy boulders. It was crystal clear except for the bits of white water scattered around. There was a cold breeze, but just enough sun peeking through the trees to make it bearable. Alley and Robert stacked two small pillars of stones on a large rock in the river. They both seemed to enjoy the short walk down and the stone-stacking equally. Of course, it wasn't long for Robert was tossing rocks towards Alley, into the water of course. Needless to say, we had a lot of fun. Mark slept the whole time.

We went on another little drive back and rested back at the cabin. Annette made chili for supper and it was fantastic. I think she kept saying, "it needs more cumin", but I thought it was great. Once Alley got up from her nap, we debated what to do next. In the process of getting ready, Robert and Annette decided to stay at the cabin and keep Mark. It wasn't long after Alley got outside in the crisp, cold air that she started saying, "It's soooo cold!" Annette took her back in and she stayed at the cabin with them. Katelin and I got the rare opportunity to go out together, so we walked quickly to The Village.

After a quick walk, listening to some Christmas music by the Pentatonix (we each shared an earbud), we made it to our destination. We didn't have long before stores would close, so we picked up two eclairs from the Donut Friar (one for Alley and one for Robert), and headed to two other shops. Next, we visited an ice cream/fudge shop and got some ice cream. I got Rush Hour which was coffee flavored with caramel swirl and chocolate chips. Let's just say I have a new favorite flavor of ice cream. Did I mention it was cold and that it was a 20-minute walk back to the cabin...and we had ice cream? I'm not sure what we were thinking, but it got cold! Katelin had her ice cream eaten by the halfway point, but I barely had half eaten by the time we got back. It was a fun time together.

We hung out together before heading to bed. I think we all had a good day today and we're looking forward to our last day or two here. I'm so glad to have family. I lived without them for a long time, but I can't imagine life without them anymore. God has truly blessed me. I'm not sure why, but I'm just going to thank him and enjoy it while I can.
