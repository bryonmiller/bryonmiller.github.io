I met with more students today and I was able to get more work graded. I still have a lot of grading to do tomorrow, but today was definitely more productive. That felt good, but I'm still a little stressed knowing what I have left. That may be part of the reason for my headache tonight. I tried drinking some water, taking a short nap, and using the massager my mom got for me. I'm not sure how much those things helped. The headache is bearable, but it is still there. For that reason, I plan to go to sleep very soon.

Katelin and I had a good day today. I hope I didn't ruin that for her with my headache and nap. I felt a little guilty when I woke up because I know she had to take care of both kids the whole time. That's not fair to her, but she does it anyway.

We talked to my mom via video call tonight. She was doing good but had missed a flight. She and the kids got to see each other and talk for a few minutes, so that was good. She's getting close to finishing with the work on her house in Hollywood. I'm not sure what she'll do next. I hope we are able to see her in person soon.

The deacons and staff at CPBC decided tonight to postpone Sunday School meetings again because of COVID. We're trying to do what we can to protect Sunday morning worship. COVID numbers are rising nationally, but we are also seeing a lot in our local area. It is getting scarier and scarier. At the same time, people need church and to be with other believers. It is hard to find the right balance and I have second-guessed our decisions a few times. I pray that God's will is done and that this virus is under control soon.
