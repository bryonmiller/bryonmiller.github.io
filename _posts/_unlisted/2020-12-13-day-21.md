I missed two days of writing. One night I was just really tired. Last night, I just forgot about it. I'm trying not to feel like I "failed" to write. It was more like I chose not to and then forgot. Friday was a good day, but a busy one. Yesterday, Saturday, we spent most of our time split between visiting with my Aunt Mary's family (at our house), Patric and Lena, and the McKinleys. It is awesome having my brother nearby, but now it is even better to have more friends in our neighborhood.

Today we had church and played the song Graves Into Gardens by Brandon Lake. It is a great song and I'm glad we finally did it. I've heard it many times, but I listened to it in the shower before church just to be sure I was comfortable with the beat of the song. About a minute into it though I was so wrapped up singing it and listening to the words that I forgot to pay attention to the beat. I was tearing up in the shower. It was a little strange, being in the shower and all, but it was a good moment of worship. I did my best to repent today. I'm still struggling with my weak faith and my desire to keep control. I feel like I need all the answers, but I don't have the time or the brainpower to get them all. Instead, I'm forced to trust in things I can't see. That is hard for me. I just hope God remains patient with me and gives me the faith I need.
