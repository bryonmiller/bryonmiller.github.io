I'm tired. This is our last night in Gatlinburg and it was a busy day. We walked along the strip a lot today. The morning started off well, but it wasn't great once we got out. I think the issue was that we were all a little tired and we weren't able to take the stroller in several of the shops. That meant one of us had to stay behind. Katelin and I are better together and it doesn't take long for me to get impatient when I'm already tired. I had a good cup of coffee, but that didn't seem to help much. It wasn't all bad and it was only for a few hours anyway. I learned that Alley likes coffee. I'm still the champ at putting Mark to sleep. I love being with my wife, even if we aren't getting along.

We had some good Italian food for lunch and I went in a few stores I wanted to visit. It was mostly a bust, but I did manage to find a new belt to replace mine that was wearing down. I also found some candy for Lena, Patric, Jarod, and Nicolis. Oh, and I got my caramel apple! We can't forget that! It's become a tradition to get a caramel apple and especially once with pecans sprinkled on it.

After a long day in town, we came back so Mark could nap. Robert, Annette, Alley, and I walked around the little creek that runs by our cabin. We picked up some rocks, threw some, and enjoyed the afternoon. It wasn't long before Robert was jumping rock to rock on the other side of the creek and Alley was trying to follow me everywhere. Next thing you know, we're throwing rocks into the water in front of each other. Alley loved it and did everything she could to splash her grandaddy. I managed to get him a little bit. She of course thought that was hilarious. After all that fun, we headed back up to the cabin.

Back outside the cabin, we built a fire in the fire pit. It was nice to sit there with family in the cool fall air listening to the creek beside us, the crackling fire in front of us, and an interchanging of two/three-part harmony singing and laughing. We ordered food to be delivered. Once it got to us, we realized a lot of it was missing. The shrimp we got was great though and we were able to get a refund for everything that was missing (and more).

Katelin is working on a nativity scene painted on small rocks she found yesterday. Her mom is painting some other rocks as well. Somewhere in between supper and the painting, we had a rowdy conversation about what to name Robert and Annettes new orange kitten. Robert insisted on Dr. Fauci and Annette thought it was ridiculous. She says it is too controversial and political. She also says it will remind her of how bad 2020 was and she doesn't want to remember that. That got me thinking about how challenging 2020 has been and yet how much we have grown. Katelin also shared some of the things she is grateful for or has learned this year. It is a good thing to think about how we have been blessed and how we have grown.

Afterward, Robert and I watched a SpaceX rocket launch. Alley fell asleep in my lap. Annette and Robert (okay, mostly Annette) packed up much of their stuff. Mark went to bed. Katelin followed. I took Alley to bed and here we are together. It has been a good trip and there are still things we could do if we had another day, but I'm ready for home. I'm ready to see my animals, find out what my mom got me for my birthday (she had it shipped and it arrives soon), and sleep in my own bed with my own pillows. Seeing the stacked rocks and thinking of home reminds me of Jacob in the Old Testament. Bethel. He used a stone for a pillow there, encountered God, anointed the stone with oil, and called the place Bethel. The House of God. Sometimes God meets us in nature, in our dreams, or in other places where we least expect Him. I hope He continues to surprise us with His presence.
