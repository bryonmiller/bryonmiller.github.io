I’m writing from my phone, so this will be short. I made it to day 10! I actually almost forgot to write tonight. I was a little more proactive today, but still not where I want to be. I think being back in the office tomorrow will help. I don’t like going back and forth like this. I can work from home exclusively and do okay, but going back and forth is tough.

We had a youth service tonight and got to play with the band. We were off last week, so we missed it. I really enjoy playing with those guys. We only did two songs, but it feels good to play and to worship. Sometimes, even though I feel distant from God, letting loose in worship just helps me forget the distance. I need to deal with it, but at the same time, I think it helps to just give Him praise. In those moments, I don’t feel so far away.

Casey and Sam close on their house Friday, so by next week we’ll be next-door neighbors! We’re excited about that and Alley will like having a little girl near her age near us. I’m glad we live in this neighborhood and that we are surrounded by people we know and trust.
