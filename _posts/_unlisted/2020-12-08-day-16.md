Sherry's dad was doing a little better last night but took a turn for the worse today. They are putting him on a ventilator and trying to get him stabilized. I can't imagine how she feels not being able to help him or even see him. I'm praying that God helps her family through this and comforts her dad in such an awful position.

Mark woke us up at 4 am with what we would best describe as a "croupy" cough and cries. I don't wake up easily, but it scared me enough that I popped up pretty quick. Katelin was taking care of him and I went back to bed. I had to get up again though to go check on him. I ended up going back to bed at about 5 am, but I took Alley into our room from her bed. I guess in my mind I couldn't do much for Mark, so I wanted to hold my other baby. I love these kids and I hate when they don't feel well. Luckily, Mark never developed a fever and seemed to get better through the day. I'm hoping he doesn't wake up coughing again tonight and that he gets some good sleep. I think that will help him out.

Katelin worked from home with me today since we kept Mark out of school. That actually went fairly smoothly since my day wasn't too hectic. I got less done than I wanted to, but that's how it goes sometimes. I'll just try again tomorrow.

I took Alley to gymnastics today and she did really well! Her cartwheels were really good considering it hasn't been a while since she could go. She got better at her handstand too. They also tried something new (looked like a heavily supported version of a back handspring) and she did better than anyone else. I was very proud of her. She had a lot of fun and tried hard.

Considering the circumstances, I'd say today was a really good day. I'm looking forward to another good day tomorrow. I'm starting to really look forward to more time off around the holidays and seeing Mark and Alley around Christmas time. I think they are going to be a lot of fun this year.
