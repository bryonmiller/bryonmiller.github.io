Happy Thanksgiving!

Today was a good day. We had a slow morning, but it picked up quick. Katelin cooked some pecan pies, mushrooms stuffed with cream cheese and bacon, and some vinegar salad. We went to her parent's house and Patric and Lena joined us. We had a great time. I prayed for our safety and I think we did a good job staying out of each others faces.

We ate and ate and ate. There was some really good food and, as rarely happens, I'm actually looking forward to some leftovers tomorrow! Alley had fun playing with her cousins. Patric and Lena seems to have a good time. I'm glad they were able to be there.

Avery came over to spend the night with Alley tonight. We found out that this is the first time she has ever spent the night anywhere other than Robert and Annette's house. She seems to have had a good time and I hope she gets some good sleep here. She told me earlier today that I was her favorite uncle. She only has two that I know of, so that isn't saying much. I also think it has a lot to do with how much she likes Alley, but hey, I'll take it!

In honor of Thanksgiving, I want to list three things that I thank God for today. Here they are in no particular order:

**Our Family.** I don't know where I would be without them. My brothers, my parents, my grandparents, aunts and uncles, cousins, in-laws, nieces and nephews, and most of all, my wife and kids. I'm truly blessed to have a big family and to be so close to so many of them. I get down about my mom being away most of the time and my dad being away too, but I had them for a long time. If nothing else, I'm happy that Katelin, Alley, and Mark are with me nearly every day.

**Our Home.** Maybe this one is a little more materialistic and maybe it comes to mind because of how tough 2020 has been, but I am truly thankful for this place where we live. So many people don't have a home and many more don't have a safe one with a warm bed. We have that and more. Even though the roof leaks, we'll have that fixed soon. Even though the pipes don't drain very well, they drain better than they used to drain. Even though the pine trees drop sap, straw, cones, and limbs on virtually everything, they sure are pretty to look at sometimes. We've had other problems with it this year, but in reality, it is a great place to live. We have friends and family near us. We aren't far from work. It has weathered several storms and should weather more. It stays cool in the summer and warm in the winter. I am grateful to be here.

**My Salvation.** I honestly can't leave this one out because without it, the other two, and a thousand more things, would be worthless. Because of Jesus Christ, I am able to live with peace and to have hope of better days no matter what I go through. One day, I'll be in heaven with Him and everything will be perfect. Not only that, but my wife and most of my closest friends and family members will be there too. I don't deserve any of that, but it was given to me as a gift. I'm so grateful for this and I hope I never take it for granted.
