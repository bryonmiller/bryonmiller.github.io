This was our first day back at church in a while because we have been out of town or there was no church for the holiday. It is always a little weird after being out of church for a bit, but it was a little stranger than normal today. At least at first. I felt sort of absent sitting there. Maybe I was just tired (upper back is sore and I have been staying up late) or maybe my mind was just other places. At any rate, things improved as the service went on and we had a great time of worship. David's message was great. He had planned on having little David speak, but he was sick. The message was on Proverbs 3. What stuck with me the most was his comment that leaning on something is the opposite of trusting in God. I lean on a lot of things including myself. Maybe that's why I have struggled spiritually lately. I pray that God will help me to trust Him more in the future.

This afternoon my neighbor's house was being cleaned out by his daughter (he passed away at the beginning of the year) and they threw away a LOT of stuff. While he was a devout atheist, the rest of his family appear to be Christians. I wonder what happened in his life for him to choose that path. I still wish I had done more to witness to him. I asked his daughter about all the old computer parts he had, but they had already thrown them out. I was hoping to make use of them for some future projects. I'm behind on my other projects anyway. Maybe I can get to work on those and reward myself with some new toys. (I've said maybe a lot in this entry!)

Tomorrow is my first day back to work in a while. I've got several emails and messages to deal with, a few assignments to grade, some announcements to send out, and some other contacts to make. I'm hoping I can get everything done tomorrow. I'm a little worried about the end of the semester and preparing for the next one. I'm doing okay in my job and I have a good reputation with my coworkers and supervisors. I just worry that I'm not doing enough or that I'm letting my students down in some way. I want them all to be successful, or at least most of them. I'm afraid that several of them will not be this semester, but I'm going to do everything I can to help them at this point. Speaking of work tomorrow, I guess I should get some sleep.
