(And Day 12 and 13...)

I missed two days. It is unfortunate as well because they were busy days. We got home late Friday after spending time with Katelin's family at Jessica's house in Pisgah. I also found out that morning that my Nana has COVID-19. Luckily, the only symptom she really has is low oxygen, although that is still serious. I just pray they treat her well and I hate that no one can see her right now. On Saturday, I got a haircut early, picked up Hardee's, cleaned out/out our shed, and helped Casey and Sam unload their trailer and vehicles. Cara and Alley played together a lot too. Later, I went over to Josh's house. We had a good time eating pizza, playing an old video game that we hadn't played in years, and watching Alabama beat LSU (55-17, Roll Tide!). We haven't really hung out much over the past few years, and this year especially, so it was good to catch up and hang out together.

Today was a good day. Despite the rising COVID cases in our area, and the number at CPBC especially, we still had church. Most people wore masks and stayed apart. Steve Alred preached since David Moorman is now quarantined. It is a strange time to be alive. I also played Cajon for Jeana Miller this morning. It was a good service, especially considering the circumstances.

After church, we ate lunch with Patric and Lena and spent some time with them. Then, Mark and Katelin took a nap while Alley and I did other things. Sam and Casey have moved in and he was setting up his dog's invisible fencing. We talked while Alley and Cara played. Alley is four and Cara is two, so I was nervous about them jumping on the trampoline together, but Alley did well with her. They had a lot of fun together and Casey finished digging out most of the outline of the fence. Lincoln, our mature dachshund, does not like their dog. I think it is more that he is afraid of him, but still. This will take some adjusting for him.

It's back to work tomorrow and I'm hoping to get a lot of work done. There are certainly a lot of things I need to get done. Katelin has a lot to do this week as well. I've been discouraged with the progress I've made, or rather haven't made, at work and at home. I'm no closer to getting my Security+ than I was a week or two ago, the new class I'm teaching in the spring is no closer to being built, and I've realized that getting my HAM license is going to be harder than I originally thought. I want to make more progress before the year is over, but at the same time, I think things will improve once the holidays are over.
