What a week! I missed the past two days. I wanted to do this daily, but it has been really difficult. This was Finals Week at NACC and grades are due on Monday. Most of my students (that are still enrolled) have passed their class, but I still had a few fail in each class. I always feel a little bit like I have failed when my students fail. It has been a busy week and I have worked hard to help students finish and pass. It is hard to watch so many just refuse to do their part and then fail as a result. I'm glad we get a little break after this because this has been stressful.

I have a student that submitted a complaint to ACCS about one of my classes. I won't go in detail here, but it was basically complaints about the content we provided (or lack thereof) and the credential he received. I've tried hard to be fair to this student and to hear him out. For whatever reason, he want contact me or respond to me directly.

I feel behind tonight. I still have so much to do before Tuesday night and more to be done before the start of next semester. I just pray that I can do these things and still spend time with my family. I know I can and I do spend time with them, but I wish I could spend more with them. I actually was having a rough moment thinking about work tonight. Then, I looked at Alley and thought, "Forget all of that. This is what matters." I hope that carries me through the remainder of this year and into next semester.
