This was my first day working from home after the break and it felt pretty weird. It wasn't a very productive day, but I did get a little work done. I think my brain has a hard time with the fact that I'm at home, not at work, and that I have been at home during the weekend. That makes it feel like a weekend at home, but it isn't. I have to actively try to distance myself from home things and focus on work things. I didn't do a very good job of that today. I was able to talk a student into catching up in my class instead of dropping, so that was a win. I wish I had gotten the grading done for my classes, so maybe I'll work on that more tomorrow.

I had an eventful time when I tried to leave to pick up Mark and Alley. I couldn't find the keys to the car anywhere. I thought maybe Katelin put them somewhere, but no luck. Then, I realized the Murano was here. I thought maybe Katelin left the keys in there, but again, no luck. I called her and she didn't answer. I started getting frustrated at this point. I found the spare key for the Murano and tried that, but the battery was dead. I finally got the battery swapped out and was able to leave. When I got to daycare to pick up Mark, I couldn't find his car seat. I picked up Alley instead and asked Katelin about the car seat. It turns out that it was at daycare, but they had moved it inside. I picked up Mark and headed home.

Luckily, despite all those issues, Katelin and I were in good moods. We went out to get food and went to Krystal's. We never go there, but Katelin likes it. Alley likes the little burgers too. We had a good night. Laid down with Alley to watch TV, but she got up and I fell asleep for a bit. Katelin worked on and off and she is still working right now. She plans to keep working for another hour or two to finish a project. It is nearly midnight and she is normally asleep before 10 pm. I told her to go to bed soon, but I'm not sure what she'll do. I love her work ethic, but I wish she didn't feel like she has to work so much from home. I'm glad she likes what she does though. She's good at it too. I just hope she doesn't get burnt out and that she isn't exhausted tomorrow. I'm glad we are together.
