My Nana is still about the same as she was yesterday. I'm glad she isn't worsening. Unfortunately, Pawpaw is now positive for COVID-19 but has no symptoms yet. Sherry's (my former supervisor) dad is still in ICU with it, but he is doing a little better and has not had to go on a ventilator. She is taking it very hard though. This thing has been hard on us and it is making for a very challenging winter. It was easy to forget in early fall how rapidly things escalated in the early spring. Now, it is all I can think about as we move through winter with increasing cases.

Work was okay today. I met with multiple students and got some grading done. I have a lot left to do this week and several things to do tomorrow in particular. I'm hoping and praying for focus and persistence tomorrow.

We went to Scottsboro's Christmas Parade tonight with Robert and Annette. It was a good break and Alley loved it. Mark wasn't too sure about it and we had to keep his ears covered most of the time. Alley racked up on candy though! She was cracking us up because, within 2-3 minutes, she had learned how to play the system. Step 1, smile and wave and the people. Step 2, say "Merry Christmas!" in a cute little voice. Step 3, wait for the rain of candy. It worked! She loaded up.

Now, I'm heading to bed. I didn't fall asleep early this afternoon, which is an improvement over the last few days. I'm hoping that I can read a little and fall asleep more easily now. I've had some trouble with that over the weekend.
