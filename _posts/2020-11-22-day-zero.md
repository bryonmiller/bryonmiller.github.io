Today, I hit the big 3-0. That's right, I'm 30. I thought maybe I would write a little since I haven't in so long.

30 is significant for many people, and for good reason. It is the start of a new decade. It was around this age that Jesus started his ministry. In light of 30, we see tones of maturity, stability, and direction. At the same time, the freedom and discovery of our 20s aren't quite lost yet. There's still time to change at 30. I have made or experienced some major changes just over this past year.

- We had a son, Mark. He will already be 8 months old tomorrow.
- Alley had turned four and continues to grow and change.
- I started teaching and then changed jobs to be a full-time Computer Science Instructor at NACC.
- Katelin and I had our first paper published in a peer-reviewed journal. I had a second paper published and it received the IACIS Best Paper Award (Research).
- COVID-19 ravaged the world and changed the economic, social, and political landscapes in the process. Perhaps even permanently in some respects.
- My mom started doing travel RN in New York, Texas, Pennsylvania, etc. working COVID-19 cases.
- Spring was a terrible time for us. We had a flea infestation. One of our first babies, Shiloh (miniature long-haired dachshund), died from heartworms even though we paid for her to have the best treatment. (While we were gone on a short trip to Weiss Lake no less.) My dad also relapsed during this time. It was a hard time, to say the least.
- There were many other smaller changes and it has been a wild ride!

Through the good and the bad, Katelin and I grew up a lot. It is amazing how much growing up you still have to do at 29! I wonder if I will say the same thing when I'm forty looking back.

## Today

We are in Gatlinburg on vacation for a few days. Katelin and I had a good morning and she ordered breakfast for me. It was a LOT of food. Alley ate about half my bacon! We went to the Dolly Parton (formerly Dixie) Stampede for their Christmas show. I wasn't even hungry by then, but I ate anyway. (I regretted that shortly after.) It always brightens my day to see Alley so excited. She loved the show and especially stomping her feet when they said, "Stampede". Mark had fun for a while, but when he got tired, he got mad. Luckily, I was able to get him to sleep. I love that little dude.

Then, we visited a shop or two, went to The Old Mill, and finally visited The Island. There we got to watch the fountain/light show, visit some other shops (Alley and I got candy), and eat some pizza. Alley really wanted to go on some rides and things, but we didn't have time to get tickets (long lines...) and it started drizzling. She still had a really good time. I love that little girl so much.

It was a great day. It's funny. There were so many people I didn't get to see. I didn't open a single present. I had no birthday cake or candles to blow out. And yet, it was one of the best birthdays I've had because I got to spend it with people I love. Thank you God for blessing me with my family.
