Today was a pretty good day. I was really been at work from about 9 am until I left at about 4:40 pm. I was meeting with students, grading assignments, answering emails, answering phone calls, etc. It felt good to get so much accomplished. Still, I had a few more things that I wish I had time to finish.

Alley is sleeping on the couch and I am sitting in the recliner. Everyone is asleep but me, even the animals. Alley is smiling in her sleep. She's a sweet girl. She went to work with Katelin today and had a really good time. I wish she could have hung out with me in my office some today, but it was just too crazy of a day. I'll get to spend some time with her tomorrow though when I take her to gymnastics.

I bought some gifts for my brothers and I hope they like them. Katelin pointed out tonight that I always put off buying them things until the last second and that it always makes me anxious. I'm not totally sure why I do that. I think I feel obligated to get them something and to take care of them. At the same time, I really want to get them something they need or want. I want them to enjoy it and appreciate it. That makes it worthwhile to me. It just takes more effort on my part to find something they will like. Nicolis was easy this year. Patric was a little harder than usual since there are things they need, especially if they have foster children soon. Jarod was typical. All in all, it wasn't that bad. I deliberated for a while though. I really do hope they like what we get them.

Now I'm going to bed. It's late and I've got a lot to do tomorrow for work. I pray God gives me focus tomorrow and helps me be efficient. Another day of being productive would go a long way to ease my stress.
