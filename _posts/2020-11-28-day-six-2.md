There were a few good games today and that’s how a lot of my time was spent. Katelin, on the other hand, worked her butt off most of the day cleaning, wrapping presents, picking up groceries, cooking, etc. I think she had a good day though. She cleaned to wood floors so well that I slipped and nearly hurt myself.

Mark is getting more mobile by the day and today he was all over the place. He’s not totally crawling, but he can roll, inch, and drag himself nearly anywhere. Alley had fun with him today and I did too. Katelin says I got attached to him today because I put him to sleep and wanted to keep holding him. I held him until he woke up. I remember doing that with Alley too. I love them both and I love holding them. I had the thought tonight that one day fairly soon Alley will be too big for me to easily carry her to her bed. I dread that day. I actually think that she will stop falling asleep away from her bed before that day comes, so maybe I can keep carrying her until then.

I’ve really been slacking since we got back from vacation. Tomorrow, even though it’s a Sunday, I will need to check in on my online classes and student messages. I also need to get back to studying for the Security+ exam and start preparing for the HAM license exam. Back to work!
